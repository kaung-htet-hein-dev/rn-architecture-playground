# Antislop Audit 001

1. **MEDIUM — Step starts timed playback instead of advancing immediately.** In `src/components/SimPanel.tsx`, the Step handler passes `stepFrom` to `clock.play`, which starts the animation-frame clock. The step therefore remains in progress until its duration elapses, contrary to the requested immediate single-step interaction. This is a functional-completeness finding under C-2.

## Follow-Up

Finding 1 was approved and resolved. Step now advances synchronously, stops active playback, and completes the final step without waiting for a timer.
