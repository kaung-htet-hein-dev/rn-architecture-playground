Motion

Full motionPackets ease across lanes over 70% of a step (2.4 s at 1×). JSI lines draw in 0.3 of a step. Lane and border colors fade over 250 ms. Dropped frames flash a red ring on the phone.
Reduced motionFollows the OS setting or the Motion toggle. Packets and lines appear at their destination with no travel. The playground cursor jumps step to step. Scroll lists move in pages, with no red flash. Chapter navigation scrolls instantly.
